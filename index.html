      Safety first

      Some Symptoms Should Stop the Check

      An online checker should never delay urgent action. Stop safely and arrange professional help when the vehicle shows an immediate safety or major-damage warning.

      !
      Stop and recover

      Do Not Continue Driving If...

      • Braking effort is seriously reduced.
      • The brake pedal sinks or feels unsafe.
      • The red oil-pressure warning remains illuminated.
      • The engine is overheating or producing steam.
      • Smoke, fuel smell or an electrical-burning smell appears.
      • Steering control or vehicle stability is seriously affected.
      • The clutch cannot transfer drive predictably.
      • Fluid is leaking rapidly.
      Arrange diagnosis promptly

      Limit Driving If...

      • A fault becomes worse under load.
      • A warning light remains illuminated.
      • A burning smell returns repeatedly.
      • Acceleration or gear selection is unreliable.
      • Coolant, oil, brake fluid or clutch fluid is reducing.
      • The fault started immediately after repair work.
      • The vehicle may not be safe for fast roads or overtaking.
      Monitor and investigate

      Careful Monitoring May Be Reasonable If...

      • The symptom is mild and not becoming worse.
      • Vehicle control remains normal.
      • No red warning light is present.
      • No fluid loss, smoke or strong smell is present.
      • Acceleration and braking remain predictable.
      • A professional check is already arranged.
      Call the emergency services when there is immediate danger

      Move away from live traffic where it is safe to do so. Do not remain beside a dangerous carriageway or attempt repairs in an unsafe position.

      UK MOT guidance

      How the Checker Handles MOT Relevance

      The app separates mechanical urgency from MOT relevance. A fault can require repair even when it is not assessed as a standalone MOT item.

      1

      Directly Relevant

      Warning lights, brakes, tyres, steering, suspension, visibility, emissions and structural defects may fall within MOT inspection categories.

      2

      Indirectly Relevant

      A fault may prevent the vehicle being operated safely during the test even when the failed component is not checked independently.

      3

      Not an MOT Guarantee

      An MOT pass confirms the test result at that time. It does not prove the vehicle has no mechanical fault or that a repair was completed correctly.

      Clear boundaries

      What AutoKnowledge Pro Cannot Do

      Trust depends on being clear about the difference between diagnostic guidance and confirmed vehicle testing.

      • It cannot communicate with the vehicle or read its ECU.
      • It cannot connect to a Bluetooth, USB or professional scan tool.
      • It cannot inspect components, fluids or wiring through a screen.
      • It cannot confirm one failed part from one symptom.
      • It cannot replace an MOT test, workshop inspection or road test.
      • It cannot guarantee that a vehicle is safe to drive.
